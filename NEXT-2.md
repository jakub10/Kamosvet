# NEXT-2.md

Supersedes the "what comes next" parts of `NEXT.md` and
`growth-and-look.md`. The design authority is still

## Correction to growth-and-look.md — apply before reading further

That document is now wrong in two places and should be edited, not
re-litigated:

- **"Flat colour, hard edge, silhouette" is no longer the target.** The
  look is atmospheric and value-led: wide lightness range, aerial
  perspective, ridges paler with distance. Flat colour survives only at
  a real silhouette edge. Quantisation is not coming back.
- **"Opposed, clash, saturated" was wrong.** The build's keyed palette
  is correct: one dominant hue on the ground, sky lighter and quieter
  beside it, the single contradiction carried by a warm light.

Everything else in that document stands, including the parts not built.

## The real gap

The world is now legible, but every place in it looks like every other
place. A walker has no reason to prefer one direction over another, and
nothing to remember a location by. That is the biggest remaining
aesthetic problem — larger than any individual shading fix.

The three items below all attack it.

## 1. Curated palettes — do this first

Cheapest beauty available. The hue wheel guarantees variety and not
taste, and some identities land on combinations nobody would choose.

Replace it with a small set of hand-made palettes — a dozen or so, each
one deliberately good, each with ground, sky, light and cover colours
chosen together. The deviation vector selects among them and may shift
within a narrow range around the chosen one, never across the whole
wheel.

This breaches nothing. The no-hand-placing rule is about the world, not
the paint.

## 2. Regions, and one broken law

Build the region lattice. It is the actual work; the law itself is a few
lines once it exists.

- **Which law: the horizon that does not behave.** The ground lifts in
  the distance into a shallow bowl, so a region has a floor and the
  walker stands inside it rather than on an endless plain. Largest
  effect of the three candidates, and the most Neverending Story.
- **Region, not person.** The law belongs to the place. Everyone there
  sees it. Since the bowl touches the surface it goes through the party
  mix, exactly as relief does — non-negotiable, no exceptions.
- **Boundaries blend narrowly.** "Held without exception" means within a
  region, not that the edge is a cliff. A narrow blend band, wide enough
  not to jar and narrow enough that the region still reads as a place.
- **One law per region, chosen by hash.** Start with the bowl and one
  neutral state (no law). More laws later, once the lattice is proven.

The point is not the bowl. The point is that places stop being
interchangeable, and that walking somewhere becomes worth doing.

## 3. Give the monument a shape

It stands correctly and it is a tapered slab. Make it one of a small set
of silhouettes chosen by hash: an arch, a staircase that ends in the air,
a trunk whose top is not visible.

Silhouette only — no detail, no geometry. At 400 m and beyond the
outline is the whole effect, which is why this is cheap.

## Not now

Trees · stranger things · traces on a server · Kamosféra identities ·
mechanics · anything on the "deliberately not done" list.

Do 1 and 3 before 2 if time is short. They are small, visible, and both
make the world better on their own. Item 2 is the one that changes what
the world is.
